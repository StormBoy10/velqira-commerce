About Velqira • Fort Kochi, Kerala

Crafted for thoughtful living,
designed to be cherished.

Founded in 2021 in the coastal heritage enclave of Kochi, Velqira celebrates the confluence of traditional South Indian metalcraft and contemporary Korean tactile aesthetics. Every creation is an individual artwork, exempt from GST under small craft atelier provisions, and handled exclusively via certified 100% prepaid UPI checkout.
